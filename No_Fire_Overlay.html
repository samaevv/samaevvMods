No Fire Overlay

No Fire Overlay is a lightweight client-side Minecraft mod that removes or lowers the fire animation on your screen when taking fire damage. Clear Visibility: Prevents fire from blocking your view, giving you full situational awareness in any combat or survival scenario. PvP & Nether Essential: Crucial for Nether exploration, lava encounters, and fast-paced PvP fights where screen clarity matters. Zero Lag: Extremely lightweight with no performance impact on your game. Note: This is a purely visual mod and does not alter fire or lava damage mechanics.
